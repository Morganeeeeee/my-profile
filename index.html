Exchange life brings more than food stops and travel memories. It also gives me ideas to explore, alongside the student research project I lead.

🔎 Research · Project lead

Data fusion for drone monitoring

I lead a student research project combining radar, optical and radio-frequency data. We explore how data fusion can improve drone monitoring while reducing reliance on expensive sensors.

Simulation prototype from our project presentation.

💡 My idea · Four-person team

Connecting students between arrivals

Departing and incoming students need each other's belongings, but their dates often do not match. I proposed a platform connecting buyers, sellers and temporary storage hosts. Our four-person team is developing this early-stage idea.

Early-stage concept, not a finished application.
